About the Statue of Unity Tour

The Statue of Unity stands at 182 metres, making it the world's tallest statue and a tribute to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. Located on Sadhu Bet island facing the Narmada Dam near Kevadia, it sits approximately 200 km from Ahmedabad, roughly 3.5 hours by road.

Spectrum Tour and Travel runs this route regularly for families, corporate groups, and school excursions. Our fleet of sedans, SUVs, and Tempo Travellers can handle any group size with the same standard of comfort and safety.
