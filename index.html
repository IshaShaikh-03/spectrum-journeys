Our Story

Born in
Ahmedabad,
Built for India

Founded on 11 November 2008, Spectrum Tour & Travel began in Ahmedabad with a conviction that Gujarat deserved a travel partner built on reliability, not promises. Starting from Sarkhej with a handful of vehicles and a focus on corporate transfers, the company expanded steadily, first across Gujarat, then nationwide.

Today, Spectrum serves over 10,000 corporate and leisure clients, operating a fleet from 4-seat sedans to 50-seat coach buses, and running pilgrimage circuits, leisure tours, and MICE operations across every major Indian destination. Common Questions

Before You
Book

How far in advance should I book for Chardham Yatra?

We recommend booking 1 to 2 months in advance for Chardham Yatra, especially for the peak April to June season. Hill routes require specialist drivers and specific vehicle classes; early booking ensures availability of the right vehicle combination for your group size.

Do you provide vehicles outside Ahmedabad and Gujarat?

Yes. We operate nationwide. Our fleet can be mobilised from Ahmedabad for outstation journeys, and we coordinate multi-city itineraries across Rajasthan, Kerala, Himachal Pradesh, Ladakh, and more.

What is included in the quoted fare?

All quotes are itemised and include driver allowance, fuel, and applicable state permit charges. Toll fees, parking, and state border taxes are billed separately and transparently. There are no hidden charges; every line item is explained before you confirm.

Are your vehicles and drivers safety-compliant?

Yes. Every vehicle carries GPS navigation. Staff buses are fitted with CCTV cameras. All drivers are uniformed, carry government-issued ID cards, hold valid commercial licences, and are registered under Motor Transport Act 173/212. Our 24/7 operations desk monitors all active trips.

Can you handle airport transfers on short notice?

We offer same-day airport transfer bookings subject to vehicle availability. For recurring corporate airport transfers, we recommend a monthly account agreement which guarantees priority scheduling. We service Ahmedabad AME and can coordinate transfers to/from Mumbai and Delhi airports.

What vehicle sizes are available for large groups?

For large groups we offer Mini Buses (20 to 32 seats), Coach Buses (35 to 50 seats), and custom multi-vehicle combinations. For conferences or pilgrimages requiring 200+ seats, we coordinate a fleet of coaches with dedicated route managers.

Do you offer corporate billing and invoicing?

Yes. Corporate accounts receive monthly consolidated invoices with route-level detail, GST-compliant billing (GST No. 24AYYPS9396M1ZW), and an assigned account manager. We currently serve companies including Mitsubishi Electric Automotive India, Nestlé India, Tata, Sysmex India, and Adani on monthly account terms.

Is the Urbania luxury bus available for weddings and private events?

Yes. The Urbania Luxury Minibus (13 to 17 seats, VIP configuration) is available for weddings, engagement functions, baraat convoys, and private events. Advance booking of 7 to 14 days is recommended for wedding dates.
