## 3. Google Search Console — So Google Finds Your Website Properly

**What it is:** Google Search Console is another free Google tool. It:
- Tells Google "hey, my website exists, please show it in search results"
- Shows you what people typed on Google before finding you
- Alerts you if there's a technical problem with your website

Without this, Google might take weeks or months to find your website on its own. With this, we can speed that up significantly.

**What I need from you:**
1. Same Gmail account as above (`traveldeskspectrum@gmail.com`)
2. Go to **search.google.com/search-console** → Sign in → Click "Add Property" → Enter `spectrumtourandtravels.in`
3. Google will ask you to "verify" that you own the website. Choose **"HTML tag"** method. It will give you a small piece of code that looks like: `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="XXXXXXXX" />`
4. Send me that code. I'll add it to the website, and then you can click "Verify" in Search Console.

> **After this is done:** I will submit your sitemap (a file that lists all your pages) to Google so it gets indexed faster.
